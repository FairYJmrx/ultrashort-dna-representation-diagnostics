\section{Data Availability}

All processed data tables, figure source summaries and analysis outputs generated for this study are maintained in the versioned GitHub repository \href{https://github.com/FairYJmrx/ultrashort-dna-representation-diagnostics/tree/release}{ultrashort-dna-representation-diagnostics}, on the \texttt{release} branch. The archived \texttt{v1.2.0} source and output snapshot is available through Zenodo at \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21882250}{doi:10.5281/zenodo.21882250}. Subsequent archived versions are linked by the Zenodo concept DOI \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21792340}{doi:10.5281/zenodo.21792340}.

Publicly reused resources include simulator- and benchmark-derived materials used for the shared-template WGS continuity audit, ART, CAMI\_TOY\_low, CAMI II marine and Dorsal motif-position analyses \citep{Huang2012ART,Sczyrba2017,Meyer2022,CliffordAdami2016Dryad}. The public release contains the 420-template continuity manifest, compressed perturbation summaries, local-triplet results, ART generation manifests, quality-stratified summaries and the public motif-position subset used in the analysis. The CAMI II marine stability probe used the public CAMI II marine short-read sample 0 reads archive and its corresponding setup archive; exact resource URLs are listed in the release repository. The release includes the streamed CAMI II marine subset used in the paired stability analysis; read-level taxonomic labels were not reconstructed from the separate truth resources.
